Strategic Recommendations

Four data-backed initiatives to reduce churn and grow retained revenue, ranked by expected impact.

$139,131 / month

Revenue currently lost to churn — the addressable opportunity for these initiatives.

01

Redesign the contract incentive structure

High Impact
Month-to-month customers churn at 42.7%, vs 11.3% for one-year and 2.8% for two-year contracts.

Customers on flexible, low-commitment plans have dramatically higher churn risk. Structuring meaningful incentives to move them to longer-term contracts addresses this gap directly.

Recommended Actions
  • Offer a meaningful discount for switching to annual billing
  • Target month-to-month customers past their 6-month mark with an upgrade offer
  • Test a price-lock guarantee for two-year contracts to boost conversion
Expected effect: Shifting even 15% of month-to-month customers to annual contracts could cut overall churn by several points.
02

Fix the support experience, not just the product

High Impact
"Attitude of support person" is the single most cited churn reason — ahead of price or competitor offers.

Customer service interactions are actively driving cancellations. This is a high-leverage fix because it can be acted on immediately without product changes or pricing adjustments.

Recommended Actions
  • Audit support call quality specifically for the highest-churn customer segment
  • Introduce a retention-focused escalation path before cancellation is finalized
  • Track support satisfaction separately from overall CSAT to catch signal early
Expected effect: Directly addresses the top voluntary churn reason, independent of pricing changes.
03

Migrate customers off manual payment methods

Medium Impact
Electronic check users churn at 45.3% — nearly 3× higher than automatic bank transfer (16.7%) or credit card (15.2%).

Friction in the billing process correlates strongly with disengagement and churn. Autopay creates "stickiness" by reducing manual renewal decisions and payment failures.

Recommended Actions
  • Offer a one-time bill credit for switching to autopay
  • Make autopay the default option at signup and renewal
  • Flag electronic-check customers for proactive outreach ahead of renewal dates
Expected effect: An easy, low-cost lever compared to pricing or contract restructuring.
04

Bundle retention-driving add-ons into base plans

Medium Impact
Customers without Tech Support churn at 41.6%, vs 15.2% for those who have it — the same pattern holds for Online Security.

Value-added services like Tech Support and Online Security create genuine product dependency and perceived protection. Getting them into customers' hands early is a cost-effective retention strategy.

Recommended Actions
  • Free 3-month trial of Tech Support and Online Security for all new signups
  • Reframe add-ons as "protection" features in all marketing and onboarding materials
  • Prioritize add-on activation campaigns for the highest-risk customer segments
Expected effect: One of the most cost-effective retention levers identified in this analysis.
